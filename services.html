CAC Business Registration Support

We guide you through the official Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) Company Registration Portal. We assist with name availability search and reservation, correct business classification under CAMA 2020, preparing required statutory documentation (Form CAC-BN01, MEMART, or CAC-IT01), and retrieving your official Status Report and Certificate of Registration.

Regulatory Note: Coach Hyela provides expert filing assistance. Formal approval and statutory issuance dates remain solely under the authority of the Corporate Affairs Commission.
